🔒 Local storage & privacy information

Your inputs, custom domains, watchlist, favourites, status notes and settings are saved in this browser on this device only. They do not sync. Clearing website data, using private browsing, or changing browsers can erase them. Export shares domain settings and quick links, not your watchlist, history, or searches. Clearing browser data may permanently erase personal activity.

❓ Help: Restore defaults & find browser storage

Fastest way to reset this builder: Open 🧹 Reset & Maintenance below, select Reset all saved data, and confirm. This restores built-in defaults for this copy of the builder, without needing to find device settings.

Back up first: Export personal links under 🕘 Recent Link History → Export History and export domains and quick links under 📦 Export / Import Settings. These are separate backups. Reset cannot be undone without them.

Where is saved information on my device? Look in your browser or device settings for Privacy & security, Site settings, Website data, Cookies and site data, Stored data, or Clear browsing data. The exact labels vary by device, browser and version. The builder uses localStorage (website storage), not traditional cookies. Clearing browsing history alone usually will not remove it.

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): Device Settings → Apps → Safari → Advanced → Website Data. You can look for the relevant site, or remove all website data. Apple instructions ↗
  • Android (Chrome): Browser ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → All sites to look for stored data, or ⋮ → Delete browsing data → More options → Cookies and site data. Google instructions ↗
  • Windows / macOS / Linux (Chrome or Edge): Browser Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings / Site data, or Delete browsing data → Cookies and other site data. Chrome instructions ↗
  • Firefox (desktop): Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data. Mozilla instructions ↗
  • Other browsers or devices: Search the browser's official help for “clear site data” + your browser + your device. Check whether you are using a normal tab, private tab, in-app browser or a downloaded HTML file.

Be careful: Clearing all cookies or website data may sign you out of unrelated websites and erase other saved data. Prefer the builder's own reset button; it targets only the builder's saved keys in the current storage area. Data from a local file:// copy may behave differently across browsers, and a different file location or browser may have separate storage.

Other fixes: Use Clear history for saved links only, Restore default links for quick links only. If the HTML file itself was edited, browser data reset will not undo those edits; open a clean original file instead.

🛡️ VPN, country & network restrictions

Access can vary by location and network. Some third-party sites may be unavailable due to country licensing, workplace/school Wi-Fi rules, ISP filtering, or local laws. A VPN may change the apparent location of your connection and encrypt traffic between your device and the VPN provider, especially useful on untrusted Wi-Fi.

Important: A VPN does not guarantee access, anonymity, protection from scams or malware, or that a site is legal or safe. The VPN provider may see connection information; choose a reputable provider, follow local laws and service terms, and avoid entering sensitive details on unfamiliar sites.
